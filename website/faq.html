WhatsApp Channel Exporter FAQ

Answers to common questions about exporting WhatsApp Channel posts and media from WhatsApp Web.

Can I export WhatsApp Channel messages?

Yes. WA Channel Exporter reads the Channel view that is open in your authorized WhatsApp Web session and packages available posts into local archive formats.

Does it download photos, videos, and audio?

Yes, when the media is available to WhatsApp Web at scan time. Media is organized in date-based folders, and unavailable or failed items are recorded in the archive report.

Does it upload my WhatsApp data?

The extension is designed for local browser processing and does not intentionally send archive contents to a project server. Review the privacy policy and source code for the current implementation.

Does it export every historical post?

Not necessarily. WhatsApp Web may not load older posts, and media URLs can expire. Scroll to load more content before scanning and use the manifest to understand what was captured.

Can I export to PDF, CSV, or Markdown?

Yes. The archive can include an offline HTML viewer, CSV, Markdown, JSONL, and media. PDF is available as a separate export scope for formatted documents.

Which browsers are supported?

The project targets Chromium-based browsers including Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, and Opera. Firefox support is not currently included.

Can I export Channels I do not follow?

No. You must be authorized to view the Channel in WhatsApp Web. The extension does not bypass login or access private material.

How do I report a problem?

Use the support page or open an issue using the repository templates on GitHub.
