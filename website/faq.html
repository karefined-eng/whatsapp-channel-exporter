WhatsApp Channel Exporter FAQ

Answers to common questions about exporting WhatsApp Channel posts and media from WhatsApp Web.

Updated September 20, 2026 · Written by karefined-eng (Founder & Lead Engineer)

Can I export WhatsApp Channel messages?

Yes. WA Channel Exporter (v1.3.1, 2026-09-20) reads the active Channel view in WhatsApp Web (see documentation for setup steps) and packages available posts into a local archive.

Does it download photos, videos, and audio?

Yes — when media is available to WhatsApp Web at scan time (v1.3.1, 2026-09-20). Files are organized by date (media/YYYY-MM-DD/); unavailable or failed items are recorded in media-report.json (see download guide).

Does it upload my WhatsApp data?

The extension runs entirely inside your browser session (local-first design; see privacy policy, 2026-09-20). It does not upload archive contents to an external server.

Does it export every historical post?

Not all historical posts load in every session. WhatsApp Web only exposes content rendered during the active session; older posts may require scrolling. Use the archive manifest (manifest.json, v1.3.1, 2026-09-20) to verify completeness.

Can I export to PDF, CSV, or Markdown?

Yes — archive formats include offline HTML viewer, CSV, Markdown (posts.md), JSONL (posts.jsonl), and ZIP. PDF export is a separate scope (v1.3.1, see PDF guide).

Which browsers are supported?

Supported browsers: Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, Opera (Chromium-based, v1.3.1, 2026-09-20). Firefox is not supported in this release.

Can I export Channels I do not follow?

No. You must be authorized to view the Channel in WhatsApp Web. The extension does not bypass login or access private material.

How do I report a problem?

Use the support page or open an issue using the repository templates on GitHub.
