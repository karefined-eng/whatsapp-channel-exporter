How to archive WhatsApp Channel updates

Use a local, documented workflow when you need to preserve Channel posts and media that you are authorized to view.

Choose an archive scope

Use Posts Only for lightweight text, Media Only for photos, videos, and audio, or All for a complete package of the content available to the scan.

Keep the archive interpretable

Retain the generated manifest.json, media-report.json, and the original ZIP alongside the rendered HTML or PDF. These files record the date range, scope, capture status, and media failures.

Use archives responsibly

Only export content you are authorized to view and save. Exporting a Channel post does not grant republication rights, and a local archive is not automatically tamper-evident or legally admissible.
